Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use our mobile application (“SpeakOn Voice commands for Tesla") available on Google Play.
1. Information Collection and Use
We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information.

When you use the Application:

No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or any other identifying credentials.
No Account Creation: The Application does not require registration, login, or the creation of an account.
No Device or Usage Data: We do not track, log, or store device identifiers (e.g., IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID), IP addresses, operating system versions, or in-app usage analytics.
No Location Data: We do not access, track, or monitor your device’s GPS or location data.
2. Device Permissions & Local Processing
Any permissions requested by the Application (such as local network access, hardware features, or local storage) are used strictly to enable features locally on your device in real time.

Any data or preferences generated while using the Application remain entirely on your device.
No data is transmitted to external servers operated by us or third parties.
3. Third-Party Services & Integrations
The Application does not include third-party advertising frameworks, tracking SDKs, or data brokers.

If your application communicates directly with third-party APIs (such as user-configured services or developer platforms) using your own credentials or API tokens, that traffic occurs directly between your device and the respective third-party service, subject to that third party's privacy policy. We do not host intermediate servers or log that communication.
4. Children’s Privacy
Because the Application does not collect or transmit any personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction), in compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.
5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly within the Application or on its Google Play Store listing. The "Last updated" date at the top of this document indicates the effective date of the latest revision.
